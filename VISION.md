# Vision and core values

## The vision

Pixel Studio is one design program, in the browser, that does what Photoshop, Illustrator and
InDesign do together. Photos, drawings and page layout live in the same place, so nobody has to
switch programs to finish a piece of work.

## Core values

1. **Beautiful on the first try.** A 10-year-old who has never designed anything can open Pixel
   Studio and make something beautiful right away, without a lesson.

2. **Grows with you.** The same program carries a beginner all the way to the most advanced
   professional work. Nobody outgrows it, and nobody has to move to another program to go further.

3. **Simple and advanced at the same time.** We do not trade one for the other. The goal is the
   most simplicity and the most advanced results together: advanced power that is easy to reach,
   never hidden behind hard ideas.

4. **Every feature earns its place.** A new feature is added only when it lets people make
   something they could not make before, or makes something easier. It must never make the app
   feel crowded or overwhelming. When in doubt, make it simpler.

5. **A 10-year-old can do anything a professional can.** Everything a real designer does in
   Photoshop, Illustrator or InDesign, a 10-year-old can do here, without learning how the program
   works first.

6. **Calm and spacious.** Using Pixel Studio should feel as quiet and open as a clear mind. Nothing
   crowds the screen or asks to be figured out, so a creative person has real freedom: all their
   thinking goes into the idea, none into the tool.

## Using these values

Before building anything, ask: does this bring Pixel Studio closer to the professional tools, and
does it keep the app just as easy and calm for a 10-year-old? If the answer to either is no, rethink it.
